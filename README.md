 Marketing Campaign Analysis – SkillCraft Technology

 Task 04 – Business Insights Report (EDA)

This project was completed as part of my **Data Analyst Internship at SkillCraft Technology**.

The objective of this task was to perform **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on a marketing campaign dataset to understand campaign performance and identify which marketing channels should receive more budget based on ROI.

 Objective

The analysis focuses on understanding:

 Which marketing channels provide better ROI?
 How do marketing channels compare in acquisition cost and conversion rate?
 What does the marketing funnel reveal about campaign performance?
 Which channels should receive more budget?

 Tools Used

 Python
 Pandas
 Matplotlib
 Jupyter Notebook

 Data Cleaning & Preparation

The dataset contains **200,005 marketing campaign records**.

Data preparation included:

 Checking for missing values
 Checking for duplicate records
 Reviewing data types
 Creating a new **Click-Through Rate (CTR)** metric
 Grouping and comparing campaign performance across marketing channels

 Exploratory Data Analysis

The analysis includes:

 Average ROI by Marketing Channel
 Average Acquisition Cost by Marketing Channel
 Average Conversion Rate by Campaign Type
 Average ROI by Location
 Marketing Campaign Funnel
 Campaign Type Distribution

 Key Insights

 **Facebook** recorded the highest average ROI among the marketing channels.
 **Website** also showed strong ROI along with relatively low acquisition cost and strong CTR.
 **Email** recorded the highest average conversion rate.
 **YouTube** recorded the highest CTR and the lowest average acquisition cost.
 **Instagram** recorded the lowest average ROI and conversion rate among the channels.
 The differences in average ROI between channels were relatively small, so the results should be used for optimization and testing rather than treated as proof of causation.

 Budget Recommendation

Based on the EDA:

 Prioritize **Facebook and Website** for increased budget allocation.
 Continue investing in **Email and YouTube** while testing campaign improvements.
 Review and optimize **Instagram** performance.
 Increase investment gradually and monitor ROI and other performance metrics continuously.

 Project Files

- `Marketing_Campaign_Analysis.ipynb` – Python EDA notebook
- `marketing_campaign_dataset.xlsx` – Marketing campaign dataset
- `Business_Insights_Report.pdf` – One-page business insights report


Conclusion

The analysis indicates that **Facebook and Website** provide the strongest ROI opportunities in the dataset. Combining ROI with conversion rate, acquisition cost, and CTR provides a more complete view of marketing channel performance and supports data-driven budget allocation.
